Privacy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

The short version. We collect only what the tools need to work: your profile if you make one, the analyses you choose to save, requests you send us, and the questions the AI answers for you. We never sell your information or use it for advertising. You can ask us to see or delete it at any time.

Who we are

BBC – Big Brain Consulting is a free online toolkit of business tools, run by BBC – Big Brain Consulting. For anything about your data, email varunshahwithai@gmail.com.

We use your information because it is needed to provide the tools you choose to use, or because we have a legitimate interest in running and improving the site. Update emails are sent only if you ask for them, and you can stop them at any time.

Who helps us run the site

  • Cloudflare hosts the website, stores the data above and provides visitor statistics.
  • Google (Gemini) answers the AI questions. Under Google's terms for users in the UK, Google does not use these questions to improve its products.

These companies may process data outside the UK. Where they do, they use the legal safeguards UK data protection law requires.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or object to how we use it. Email varunshahwithai@gmail.com from the address you used and we will reply within one month. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Children

The tools are designed for businesses and adults. They are not intended for children under 18.

About the tools

The tools give estimates to help you think through decisions. They are not financial, legal or professional advice. AI-generated values are estimates to check, never facts.

Changes

If we change how we use your information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
