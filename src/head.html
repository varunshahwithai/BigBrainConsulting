Pricing and elasticity

When price changes, demand changes too. This is where you see by how much, and what it does to profit.

Price elasticity

Price elasticity measures how sensitive demand is to changes in price.

  • Below −1: demand is relatively price sensitive (elastic)
  • Exactly −1: demand changes in proportion to price (unit elastic)
  • Between −1 and 0: demand is relatively price insensitive (inelastic)

This is an assumption, not a prediction. Test a few values to see how much your decision depends on it. The model uses: % change in volume = elasticity × % change in price.
