Step 2 of 8

Gather the evidence

Research the company online, upload documents you have, or both. Everything you collect lands in one place, with its source.

Option A

Research it online

Gemini searches Google for annual reports, filings and other public information. Each run takes about 20 to 60 seconds.

Option B

Upload documents

Annual reports, accounts, investor presentations, management accounts or spreadsheets. PDF, Excel or CSV, up to about 19 MB each.

We don't keep your files. Only the figures read from them are saved, with the file name and page numbers. Files are sent to Google's Gemini to be read, so don't upload anything you don't have the right to share.
